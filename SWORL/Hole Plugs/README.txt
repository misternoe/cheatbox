/////////////
PRINTER SETTINGS
/////////////

Filament type: TPU only! Harder materials risk cosmetic damage to the finish of your controller!

Infill: Gyroid, 40%

Layer Height: 0.2mm minimum

Printing orientation: Print face down

Build Plate Adhesion: Glue is recommended for easier removal

Supports: None



/////////////
INSTALLATION
/////////////
Above Artwork Version:
Acrylic top plate should be on. Carefully push down into place

Below Artwork Version:
Remove artwork then push into hole
