///////////////////////////////
THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE PRINTING
///////////////////////////////

I have not tested this on an actual controller. All testing was conducted on a printed step file from Mavercade. Assuming I didn't mess up the scaling, this should work fine!

Materials tested:
PLA: Excellent output

Important:
Make sure that your 3D printer is configured properly before printing this accessory. Due to the thin nature of this product, ensuring that the first few layers print cleanly will ensure a stronger end product.


/////////////
PRINTER SETTINGS
/////////////
20% infill or higher recommended

Printing orientation
You can print flat for a faster print, or face down to make use of built plate texture. 

Build Plate Adhesion Type
Mouse ears or brim highly recommended

Supports
Highly recommend use of a support material if printing face down! Printing face up requires no supports, but I would recommend adding support to the screw holes for aesthetics.



/////////////
INSTALLATION
/////////////

USAGE AS A TRAY EXTENDER:
You can simply pop your Keebfighter 02 into the FOOTSIE and your are good to go

OR SCREW IT INTO YOUR CONTROLLER:
Unscrew the bottom screws from the controller and run them through the bottom of the FOOTSIE into the controller. I have not tested this myself, so you may need to get longer screws!
